Our Story

From a rainy afternoon in Fort Kochi to the mandapam at Guruvayur

  1. 2019

    Where it began

    ആദ്യ കാഴ്ച

    We met as volunteers at the Kochi-Muziris Biennale. One shared umbrella during a Fort Kochi downpour, and we kept talking long after the rain stopped.

  2. 2021

    The first trip

    ആദ്യ യാത്ര

    A "coincidental" weekend in Munnar with friends. Tea estates, cold mist, kattan chaya at every stop, and Meera beating everyone at cards.

  3. 2024

    Pennukaanal

    പെണ്ണുകാണൽ

    Our families met over chaaya and unniyappam at Meera's home in Thrissur. By the second cup, both mothers had already started planning.

  4. 2026

    Nischayam

    നിശ്ചയം

    Rings exchanged before the lit nilavilakku. The grandmothers fixed the muhurtham before the payasam was served.
